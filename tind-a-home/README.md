# Tind-A-Home

Our third-ranked solution prototype (IP Group 14: Landen Heine, Ammi Meyer, Kellan Nash).
It's a dating-app take on Find-A-Home: families swipe through dementia care homes one at a time, the way they'd swipe on Tinder.

**Open `index.html` in any browser**, or run `npm start` from the repo root and open http://localhost:3000/tind-a-home/. You don't need a server or an install. Everything you enter is saved in that browser's localStorage.

## What it does
- **Intake (about 2 minutes, one question per screen):** who's searching, the loved one's profile, dementia stage and type, behaviors (wandering, exit-seeking, aggression, sundowning, falls), mobility and medical needs, payment and budget, ZIP code, distance and timeline, and interests.
- **Swipe deck:** best fit first. Drag right or press ♥ to **Match**, drag left or press ✕ to **Pass**, drag up or press ★ to **Save**, and press ↺ to undo. The arrow keys work too. Tap a card to see the home's full profile.
- **Every card answers the 5 questions from our interviews:** Is it safe? Can we pay? Dementia care? Is it close? Is there room?
- **It's a Match!** screen, then a **Matches** list with tour status (Matched → Tour requested → Toured → Top choice), side-by-side **Compare** for up to 3 homes, and **Share with family**.
- **Tour planner** for each match: a pre-written call/email message, a tour checklist (with questions based on the loved one's interests), and notes.
- **Saved** (maybe-later) list, a **Profile** page with edit, discovery filters and passed homes, a **What to do next** guide, and free local help (Alzheimer's Association, Aging Partners, iServe Nebraska, Care Compare).

## Data
69 real nursing homes and memory care communities around Lincoln and Omaha. The data comes from the Find-A-Home prototype (`../find-a-home/data`), which includes Medicare/CMS inspections, star ratings and quality measures, plus curated memory-unit and payment information.
`js/data.js` is generated by this script. Rebuild it after the Find-A-Home data changes (or run `npm run build:tind` from the repo root):

```bash
node scripts/build-data.js
```

Building images are drawn illustrations, not photos of the real homes. Prices marked with * are area averages.
