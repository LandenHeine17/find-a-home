IP Group 14 · Prototypes

Three ways to help a family find dementia care

Each prototype uses the same 69 real nursing homes and memory care communities around Lincoln and Omaha, Nebraska. Pick one to try it.

Napkin sketch #1

Find-A-Home

A calm, website-style search. Answer a few questions, then filter, compare and save the places that fit.

  • Ranked matches with filters for price, distance, safety and Medicaid
  • Detail pages with Medicare ratings and inspections
  • Side-by-side compare, saved list and tour requests
Open Find-A-Home
Napkin sketch #2

Map-A-Home

Every home on a map, colored by how well it matches your loved one, with a chat guide that builds the profile for you.

  • Pins show each home's match %, red to green
  • Care Guide chat asks questions and answers them
  • Game-style profile, hearts and tour booking
Open Map-A-Home
Napkin sketch #3

Tind-A-Home

A dating-app take on the search: swipe through homes one at a time, best fit first.

  • Swipe right to match, left to pass, up to save
  • Every card answers: safe? affordable? dementia care? close? room?
  • Matches list, compare, tour planner and share with family
Open Tind-A-Home

These are student prototypes for user testing. Facility info comes from public sources (Medicare Care Compare, Nebraska licensing lists) and may be out of date. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; each prototype saves your answers in this browser only.
